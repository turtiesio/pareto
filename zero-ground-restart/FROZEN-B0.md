# ZERO GROUND restart — frozen builder candidate B0

Freeze date: 2026-09-01

This candidate was derived without reading `../ground.md`. It is a falsifiable
bounded experiment, not an architecture claim. No name below is promoted to a
system primitive merely because it appears in the test contract.

## Result before construction

The broad goal is not yet a concrete external contract. It does not fix the
legal boundary traces, permitted future experiments, observation equality,
failure/nondeterminism model, evolution envelope, human tasks, resource bounds,
or realizations. Therefore no particular physical bit is unconditionally
necessary. A constant contract needs no history state; a future language able
to ask any predicate of the past forces literal-history equivalence.

The first experiment consequently freezes one small contract and states every
claim relative to its bounds.

## Contract-independent frame

A contract instance is `(Sigma, L, K, P, R, E, G)`:

- `Sigma`: exact, framed, directed boundary marks;
- `L`: the prefix-closed language of legal finite histories;
- `K(h)`: permitted adaptive future experiments after `h`;
- `P`: declared observation projections/viewers;
- `R(h,k,p,e,g)`: required observable behavior (a value, trace set,
  distribution, or strategy as the contract declares);
- `E`: quantified environment, failure, clock, durability, dependency, and
  resource assumptions;
- `G`: permitted contract evolution and migration operations.

Anything allowed to affect a future obligation must be in the boundary
history, fixed by the contract, or explicitly quantified in `E`/`G`. Illegal
continuations produce the observable value `disabled`.

Two histories are equivalent exactly when all jointly permitted adaptive
contexts, viewers, restart schedules, environments, evolutions, and
realizations require equal observations. A usable incremental quotient must be
a right congruence under every legal next boundary mark.

## Bounded contract C0

The sample protocol is serial. An inbound frame is accepted only when no
outbound frame is owed. Restart may occur after every crossing, including after
an inbound request and before its owed output.

Finite values are source labels `a,b`, payloads `0,1`, action keys `k,l`, and
interpretations `R(u,0,1)`, `R(u,0,0)`, and `R(v,0,1)`. The first two differ on
an input not immediately exercised; the first and third compute alike but have
different explanatory labels. The initial interpretation is `R(d,0,1)`.

Inbound frames are:

- `P(source,bit)`: author a labeled payload;
- `R(label,on0,on1)`: select an interpretation;
- `O`: observe the latest authored pair;
- `Q`: obtain its interpreted value;
- `X`: explain the current result;
- `A(key)`: request a boundary action;
- `ACK(key)`: acknowledge a crossed action;
- `S(key)`: inspect boundary-action status.

The raw-trace oracle, independently of any candidate encoder, requires:

- `P` and `R` are silent.
- `O` owes `EMPTY` or `RAW(source,bit)`.
- `Q` owes `EMPTY` or `VAL(mapped-bit)`.
- `X` owes `EMPTY` or `WHY(source,bit,rule-label,mapped-bit)`.
- The first `A(key)` with content freezes the current descriptor and owes
  `DO(key,descriptor)`. Repetition while pending owes the same `DO`.
- `ACK(key)` is legal only after its `DO` crossed. Later `A(key)` owes
  `ALREADY(key,descriptor)`.
- `S(key)` owes `ABSENT`, `PENDING`, or `DONE`, including the descriptor where
  present.
- `A(key)` without content owes `NO_DATA` and creates no durable status.
- `resume` emits an owed output, if any.

Crossing `DO` is the action promised by C0. No exactly-once physical effect
beyond that boundary is claimed.

Enumerate all legal boundary prefixes generated by at most four inbound frames,
restart at every prefix, and all legal futures generated by at most two inbound
frames with all forced-output/resume schedules. Compare exact client-port and
action-port traces plus legality. Unknown oracle cells remain `UNKNOWN`, never
equal by default.

## Frozen candidates

Let `Sig(h)` be the ordered vector of every bounded legality/observation result.
The searched quotient groups histories with equal residual signatures and is
then refined to stability under legal one-step extensions.

Candidate A — quotient ordinal:

- canonically order quotient classes by their least histories;
- persist `(specification digest, class rank)`;
- use a generated transition/output table after restart.

Candidate B — canonical representative:

- persist `(specification digest, shortest lexical history in the class)`;
- replay it through the independent oracle on restart;
- after a crossing, canonicalize to the new class representative.

The representative is a synthetic class name, never historical provenance.
The digest-addressed specification must remain available; a digest by itself
does not supply it.

Candidate A may use a fixed-width nonvolatile word plus ROM/table. Candidate B
may use self-delimiting marks on a sequential filesystem, QR/optical medium, or
punched medium plus a replay machine. An abstraction map to the same quotient,
not physical similarity, establishes equivalence.

## Mechanical falsification

1. Generate the bounded histories and adaptive futures; do not hand-pick only
   convenient examples.
2. Execute the raw-trace oracle and compute every `Sig(h)`.
3. Group histories by each restarted candidate encoding.
4. Report any candidate cell containing distinct signatures.
5. Order witnesses by total history crossings, edit distance, future depth,
   first divergent observation, then lexical tie-break; exhaust that order.
6. Explore every declared deletion/merge branch, not one greedy path.
7. Accept derivation/recomputation only with a total deterministic recipe, its
   exact available specification, clean-restart conformance, and resource test.
8. Keep all sound irreducible candidates and compare them by a partial order.

The output must separately test encoding soundness and decoded behavior. A
one-to-one but wrong decoder is still wrong.

## Exact scope of the freeze

B0 does not claim global minimality. It leaves unsupported: unbounded domains;
arbitrary bytes; malformed framing; concurrency/reordering; time/expiry;
authorization/confidentiality; physical effects beyond `DO`; failures inside
capture machinery; indefinite key retention; resource deadlines; and contract
changes that split an old quotient class.

The strongest permitted conclusion is conditional: within the frozen finite
scope, after exhaustive enumeration and declared transformations, a class merge
with a minimized witness establishes a responsibility. Absence of a bounded
witness does not establish global forgettability.
