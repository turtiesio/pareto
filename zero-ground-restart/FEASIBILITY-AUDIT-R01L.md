## 0. Decision and claim boundary

FIRST MILESTONE: FAIL / NOT ACHIEVED.

R0.1L is materially cleaner than the preceding research shape and yields useful
contract-relative local results: accepted labeled effect occurrences and their
multiplicity, plus the particular causal placements exposed by the recorded
witnesses, must survive. Actor spelling and replay views have named derivations

the residual `ZG-1` future contract. These results do not define the complete
quotient or an exact global partition.

The frozen first-milestone statement nevertheless fails for three independent
document-level reasons:

1. **ORACLE is not total.** A valid history of 9,362 accepted `WA0x` effects
   directly from the semantic root needs a nested branch-item length of 65,542,
   exceeding its U16 item length and raising `ValueError`. This falsifies
   ORACLE as an all-`ZG-1` encoding. It does **not** falsify the separate
   PROJECTED3 sufficiency induction.

   required.** At freeze it lacked the demanded extracted-code artifact,
   interpreter digest/version, complete stdout/stderr, exit status, and machine
   manifest. The later experiment now supplies a reproducible D/D1 run record,
   but it cannot retroactively turn the seed's earlier self-credit into frozen
   evidence.
3. **Two positive MAY REBUILD entries were underdefined.** Generic
   indexes/caches/query plans/navigation views and a hypothetical canonical
   partial-order ordering had no named reconstruction algorithm or artifact
   specification. They are UNKNOWN, not positive rebuild results. The exact
   contract/version binding required by every valid rebuild remains charged as
   external specification/TCB machinery, although it is not itself a
   quotient-derived history item under fixed `ZG-1`.

The later executable reports **5 PASS / 1 FAIL / 7 UNKNOWN**. It reproducibly
executes D and the declared D1 domain, corroborates actor reconstruction over
valid D1 projections, and reproduces the ORACLE boundary. It is an
oracle/falsifier, not a subject, persistent realization, architecture, physical
experiment, complete quotient, or global-minimality proof.

The independent post-audit scope review also corrects four promotions in the
first integrated draft: the archive was misquoted about D1; the external

No scalar score, percentage, rank, weight, or compensating aggregate is used.
