# ZERO GROUND R0.1N — integrated feasibility audit

## 0. Decision and exact claim boundary

**FIRST MILESTONE: FAIL / NOT ACHIEVED.**

R0.1N produces one useful but conditional result. If an exact raw-audit
contract fixes common snapshot-bearing requests, request/response capture, and
byte-exact observations, then enough information to reconstruct the complete
ordered occurrence sequence is one quotient-derived **MUST SURVIVE**
responsibility. Count, offsets, and other named deterministic folds may
rebuild. Transient machinery that neither crossed nor can affect a future may
forget.

Frozen C01 does not supply that antecedent. Its typed requests and responses
must themselves enter history, but their bytes, directions, ordering, and
snapshot timing are undefined. E01 deliberately lacks its opcode table,
binary grammar, `mode`, and executable RUN behavior. Consequently the complete
future domain and equivalence relation cannot be executed, and no unconditional
persistence partition follows.

Three distinctions govern this verdict:

1. **Abstract P01 parsing survives.** Given an already-existing finite P01 byte
   string with its total extent and the fixed codec, canonical parsing is
   injective. No mathematical P01 collision was found or established.
2. **The frozen total contract fails.** Empty history plus one typed request is
   already underdefined, and the executable futures are Python projections
   rather than common captured C01 requests or E01 programs.
3. **Physical completion is absent.** No subject, durable medium, recovery run,
   human result, or unlike-realization pair exists. Literal unbounded-domain
   conformance is additionally impossible for a closed finite-state
   realization under the five explicit antecedents in Section 6.

The pinned audit reports exactly **6 PASS / 3 FAIL / 11 UNKNOWN**. Those are
runner labels, not a scalar score. In the integrated semantic accounting, its
F02 result is an imprecise “self-delimiting history word” wording failure and
an EOF/container externalization finding—not a P01 collision or an independent
codec-functionality failure once a byte-string extent is supplied.

This audit does not choose a representation, constructor, field, object,
record, log, graph, state machine, layer, program, package, storage medium, or
architecture.

## 2. Boundary contract and future equivalence

### 2.1 Unconditional abstract result

For an already-given finite sequence of `(direction,payload)` pairs and an
already-bounded finite byte-string value:

- a P01 direction byte is `00` or `01`;
- shortest ULEB fixes the next payload length;
- that length fixes the next frame boundary; and
- iteration to the supplied input end recovers at most one sequence.

This proves mathematical P01 injectivity in that abstract domain. It says
nothing by itself about permitted futures, persistence, physical EOF,
corruption, availability, or global minimality.

Occurrence count, P01 offsets, last occurrence, direction counts, and other
named folds are deterministic functions of the exact sequence and their
identified algorithms. This is unconditional derivability, not evidence that
the source or computation survives restart.

### 2.2 Smallest missing-transition pressures

From empty history, one `LENGTH(0)` request must cross and its response must
cross. C01 supplies no canonical request payload, response payload, direction,
capture order, or rule relating snapshot resolution to request capture. The
next required history is therefore not unique.

From empty history, one `CROSS(0,empty)` is likewise ambiguous. `CROSS` says it
appends exactly one occurrence, while every typed request and response that
crosses must also be captured. The contract does not say whether the request
is identical to the requested occurrence or additional traffic, nor whether a
response exists.

These witnesses are minimal only in the leading coordinates
`(initial occurrences, future requests) = (0,1)`. Exact argument-byte and
lexical minimality cannot be computed because the missing serializer is the
object under attack. They are missing-definition witnesses, not history-pair
collisions.

### 2.3 Harness correspondence failure

For `h0=()` and `h1=((0,empty),)`, the executable function named `LENGTH`
returns `(0,1)`. A fixed pre-capture snapshot request instead gives:

```text
LENGTH(0) -> (0,0)
LENGTH(1) -> (REJECTED,1)
LENGTH(2) -> (REJECTED,REJECTED)
```

Thus the executable selected a history-dependent current snapshot, not one
common C01 continuation. Its `AT` functions also omit `snapshot`; APPEND
directly concatenates tuples without captured request/response traffic; and
view, interpretation, action, and explanation functions are direct Python
projections rather than encoded E01 programs.

The bounded signatures remain useful for falsifying lossy functions of an
abstract sequence. They are not executions of complete C01 future behavior.

### 2.4 Conditional identity theorem

If one fixed extension supplies exact request/response bytes and order, exact
snapshot timing, and total common raw observations, then:

- for equal-length histories whose first difference is at index `i`, common
  `AT(snapshot=n,index=i)` returns different occurrences; and
- for lengths `n1<n2`, common `LENGTH(snapshot=n2)` returns `REJECTED` versus
  `n2` under pre-request snapshot binding.

Under that antecedent, `h1 ≡ h2` iff the occurrence sequences are equal. The
identity quotient and the persistence classifications in Section 4 are
conditional on that theorem. Frozen C01 itself does not complete the
antecedent, so complete captured-traffic equivalence remains UNKNOWN.

### 2.5 Required semantic surface is missing

E01 intentionally omits its numeric opcode table and program binary grammar.
`mode` has no domain or semantics. Program/result encodings, numeric edge
domains, SLICE boundaries, COMPARE prefix endings, and several resource cases
are not total. No frozen executable implements RUN, buffered action/view
crossings, or machine traces.

This is decisive because interpretation, querying, action, explanation, and
program-based evolution are advertised required futures. A caller convention,
prompt, runtime, or future implementation would externalize the missing
semantics; it cannot be credited as zero machinery.

### 3.3 F02 and outer extent

For `e=((0,empty),)`:

```text
encode(e)                 = 0000
encode(e) || encode(e)    = 00000000
encode(e || e)            = 00000000
```

P01 frames do not delimit one complete history word from adjacent material.
The candidate's “self-delimiting history word/representation” wording is too
strong, and a physical container extent or EOF is required. This is not
`encode(h1)=encode(h2)` for distinct histories when each finite byte-string
value already includes its extent. F02 is therefore a wording/externalization
finding, not a P01 collision or a third independent codec failure.

### 3.4 Integrity pressure

The smallest exercised coherent one-bit change is:

```text
0000 -> 0100
(0,empty) -> (1,empty)
```

Both endpoints are canonical P01. Whole-frame truncation can similarly turn
`0000` into the valid empty word. Malformed-input rejection and round-trip do
not detect valid-to-valid alteration or rollback. No retained integrity or
freshness anchor, physical subject, or recovery experiment exists.

This is a storage-channel transition, not an encoder collision. The candidate
correctly leaves corruption and recovery UNKNOWN; its narrower phrase that raw
retrieval makes loss detectable requires an independent expected value.

## 4. Post-attack persistence classification

### 4.1 Unconditional status

Because frozen C01 does not determine the future transition, no unconditional
history-quotient MUST SURVIVE or execution-level MAY FORGET classification is
defended. The unconditional results are only abstract P01 injectivity with
supplied extent and mathematical derivability from an already-given sequence.

### 4.2 Conditional MUST SURVIVE

Under the exact raw-audit antecedent in Section 2.4, there is one normalized
quotient-derived responsibility:

> Enough information must survive to reconstruct the exact finite ordered
> sequence of boundary occurrences.

This entails recoverable sequence extent, occurrence boundaries, order,
multiplicity, every direction, every payload extent, and every payload byte.
They are aspects of one sequence, not separate fields or duplicate persistence
charges.

| forced aspect | displayed pair | conditional common future |
|---|---|---|
| sequence extent | `()` / `((0,empty),)` | `LENGTH(1)` -> `REJECTED` / `1` |
| direction | `((0,empty),)` / `((1,empty),)` | `AT(1,0)` |
| empty/one-byte payload | `((0,empty),)` / `((0,00),)` | `AT(1,0)` |
| payload value | `((0,00),)` / `((0,01),)` | `AT(1,0)` |
| occurrence boundary | `((0,00),)` / `((0,empty),(0,empty))` | `AT(1,0)` |
| multiplicity | one/two `((0,empty))` occurrences | `LENGTH(2)` -> `REJECTED` / `2` |
| order | `((0,empty),(1,empty))` / reverse | `AT(2,0)` |

The conditional futures assume the stated pre-request snapshot rule. They are
translations of bounded witnesses, not frozen C01 traces.

If nondeterministically chosen bytes actually cross, those bytes are already
occurrences within this exact sequence. R0.1N instantiates no model, clock,
random source, service, or human producer and forces no additional provenance
responsibility.

### 4.3 Conditional MAY REBUILD

Given the exact sequence plus each named, available specification:

- occurrence count and exact named folds;
- P01 offsets, direct indexes, and navigation tables;
- last occurrence and direction counts;
- P01 bytes or another identified reversible encoding; and
- deterministic projections that did not themselves cross and have an exact
  algorithm/version

may rebuild. The bundle experiment exercises only count, last, and
direction-count deletion. Rebuild moves cost to source/specification
availability, scanning, CPU, memory, latency, validation, cache construction,
and TCB.

Generic hashes, views, policies, evidence associations, and configuration do
not receive a blanket verdict. E01 traces and installed policy state lack an
exact grammar/lifecycle and remain UNKNOWN. A recomputed digest over corrupted
bytes cannot reconstruct a lost historical integrity reference.

### 4.4 Conditional MAY FORGET

Only distinctions that never crossed, cannot influence a permitted future,
and are past their exposure window may forget, including:

- a completed decoder cursor;
- temporary evaluator cells after all required outputs cross;
- cache eviction order; and
- host allocation identities, scheduling choices, and clocks excluded from
  the boundary.

No occurrence distinction may forget under the conditional identity quotient.
Because capture, crash, and RUN are incomplete, operational eligibility for
these deletions remains conditional.

### 4.5 External machinery and UNKNOWNs

The fixed C01/P01 specification, decoder selection, capture rules, E01 rules,
and EOF/container extent are required total-system machinery, not
quotient-derived history state. Their availability, distribution, binding, and
correct execution remain charged to the TCB.

Boundary capture, E01/RUN, causal/evidentiary typing, integrity/freshness,
crash/effect coordination, human semantics, service levels, evolution,
portability, and unlike realizations remain missing or UNKNOWN. They are not
silently converted into fields or positive MAY REBUILD results.

## 6. Mathematical totality and physical realizability

### 6.1 Mathematical P01 totality

For every already-given mathematical finite history, every payload has a
finite natural length, shortest ULEB exists, and finite concatenated frames
parse uniquely when the total input extent is supplied. The Python runner is
bounded and does not establish host execution for all such histories.

### 6.2 Conditional finite-state impossibility

The archive's pigeonhole result is valid only when all five antecedents hold:

1. the closed total realization—including side services, people, media, and
   restart influences—has `Q_R` durably distinguishable restart states;
2. every finite `CROSS(d,p)` is mandatory and succeeds;
3. no capacity refusal, exhaustion, reset, loss, hang, or alternative result is
   permitted;
4. after restart, one total common byte-exact observation must distinguish the
   accepted occurrence under completed capture/snapshot semantics; and
5. caller/environment memory is not an undeclared survivor outside `Q_R`.

For that realization define:

```text
m_R = min { m >= 0 : 2 * 256^m > Q_R }.
```

There are more one-occurrence direction/payload histories of length `m_R` than
restart states. At least two histories merge, or a mandatory input is refused
or fails. A common raw observation distinguishes the histories.

This is a conditional parametric impossibility theorem, not a fixed numeric
witness, empirical physical evidence, or P01-byte collision. If “accepted
continuation” allows capacity rejection, C01 never defines that rejection's
boundary result and is underdefined instead. Extending the theorem to E01 is
further conditional on a complete grammar capable of its advertised unbounded
values and buffers.

Physical durability/recovery and unlike-realization evidence remain UNKNOWN.
The theorem shows that a literal all-domain claim cannot be assigned to a
closed finite-state realization under the stated antecedents.

## 8. Archive replay disposition

The quarantined archive adds no mathematical P01 collision. For the
missing-wire family, it adds no witness with fewer than the established
leading `(initial occurrences, future requests) = (0,1)` coordinates; byte
and lexical minimality remain undefined. It adds no smaller fixed snapshot,
semantic-substitution, or one-bit-corruption witness under their stated
metrics. It corroborates current delimiter/extent, evidence, snapshot,
action/completion, terminal/progress, viewer/policy, corruption/recovery,
evolution, cognition, navigation, TCB, and unlike-realization pressures.

Terminal, viewer permissions, physical application/completion, installed
policy, and cross-version action semantics are blocked by absent capabilities.
That is unsupported scope, not PASS and not zero complexity.

The archive contributes one stronger family: the conditional finite-state
all-domain impossibility in Section 6. It must not be restated as unconditional
physical evidence, and its final archive classification must not promote fixed
specification or EOF into the history quotient.

## 9. Final feasibility statement

R0.1N is a valuable clean falsification round because it makes a strong raw
audit idea precise enough to expose where “store the transcript” hides the
rest of the system. It establishes abstract P01 injectivity with supplied
extent and useful bounded collision witnesses. It does not establish an
unconditional history quotient or the smallest total system.

The defensible between-execution statement is therefore explicitly
conditional:

- **MUST SURVIVE:** under a completed exact raw-audit boundary, enough
  information to reconstruct the exact ordered occurrence sequence, forced by
  the displayed common raw observations.
- **MAY REBUILD:** only named deterministic folds/materializations from that
  exact sequence plus an exact, available specification, with runtime and TCB
  charged.
- **MAY FORGET:** only transient, non-crossing, future-irrelevant machinery
  after its exposure window, under completed execution semantics.
- **EXTERNAL / UNKNOWN:** fixed specifications and EOF are required machinery
  outside the quotient; capture, E01, integrity, operations, humans,
  navigation service levels, evolution, portability, physical durability, and
  unlike realizations remain missing or UNKNOWN.

Because the antecedent is not frozen and the required total-system capabilities
are absent, this is not the requested first milestone.

**FIRST MILESTONE: FAIL / NOT ACHIEVED.**
