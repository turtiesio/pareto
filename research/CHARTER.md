# ZERO GROUND research charter

Started: 2026-09-01

## Research question

Discover the smallest **total system** in which meaning about arbitrary reality can be preserved, understood, authored, checked, derived, acted on, explained, evolved, and realized economically across unlike physical systems.

This repository begins without an inherited architecture. Names such as object, fact, claim, graph, event, relation, rule, type, actor, and program are hypotheses until a deletion, merge, derivation, and collision test shows otherwise.

## Hard requirements

The result must attempt to satisfy all of the following together:

1. Meaningful things can become addressable, inspectable, versionable data, while opaque physical execution may remain outside the semantic boundary.
2. Humans normally author and review natural domain concepts, not a machine normal form.
3. Mechanically knowable bad authored meaning is rejected; observed broken reality is retained and diagnosed.
4. Unknown future data is preserved without invented interpretation and without accidental authority.
5. Meaning is independent of storage. A target that cannot provide a required guarantee reports `UNSUPPORTED` rather than weakening it silently.
6. The semantic kernel stays tiny only when the total burden also stays small.
7. Identity does not absorb mutable context.
8. Mechanically derived meaning is not independently authored truth. Materialization is disposable representation.
9. Humans can inspect arbitrarily large worlds through bounded, task-appropriate projections.
10. Identity evidence, authority, action authorization, and information disclosure are expressible without eternalizing current mechanisms.
11. `Tenant`, `User`, `Role`, `AWS`, and other contemporary vocabulary are not universal primitives without minimized witnesses.
12. New domains normally extend data, rules, surfaces, compilers, or adapters rather than the kernel.
13. A future implementation can reconstruct intended meaning after the original runtime and organization disappear.

## Order of judgment

1. Preserve distinctions required for correct decisions.
2. Detect invalid interpretation where mechanically possible.
3. Enforce claimed guarantees honestly.
4. Preserve required provenance, history, conflict, and unknown state.
5. Then optimize cognition, authoring, query, runtime, storage, operations, trust, evolution, portability, explanation, and loss risk.

No weighted scalar score is permitted. Candidates remain on a Pareto frontier unless dominated on every relevant dimension subject to the hard requirements.

## Mandatory research discipline

- Each round is append-only. A correction is a later record, never a rewritten history.
- Every proposed primitive receives deletion, merge, derivation, and minimized-collision tests.
- Complexity moved into opaque data, callbacks, scripts, prompts, adapters, runtime magic, or tribal knowledge is still counted.
- Implementation is falsification evidence, not the product goal.
- Performance claims are labeled measured, analytically modeled, estimated, or assumed.
- Builder reasoning is challenged by an independent breaker. Human-facing models are tested by reviewers who did not see their formal derivation.
- A frozen candidate needs a fresh-domain transfer, multiple authoring surfaces, two unlike realizations, explicit TCB and cost evidence, and three additional clean adversarial rounds before convergence.

## Round record

Every `rounds/NNNN/` directory records:

1. question;
2. current and competing candidates;
3. minimized counterexample sought;
4. executable model;
5. tests;
6. mutation attacks;
7. cognitive test;
8. cost evidence;
9. deletion attempt;
10. result;
11. Pareto movement;
12. highest-information next experiment.

## Prime question

After every apparent simplification ask: **Where is the complexity now?**
