# Known limits and open boundaries

As of the start of Round 0001:

- “Arbitrary reality” cannot mean lossless capture of the physical universe. A capture system preserves only evidence it actually samples, plus explicit knowledge about coverage where available.
- Exact bytes do not preserve unrecorded physical context, intent, causality, or truth.
- Unknown data can be retained but cannot safely authorize action merely by being retained.
- No serialization is timeless by itself. Longevity requires a format definition, algorithms, test vectors, and an interpretable migration chain.
- No storage-independent contract can force an incapable backend to provide atomicity, confidentiality, durability, or complete enumeration.
- Human comprehensibility is not implied by a small formal model and has not yet been tested.
- Universal semantics may be impossible without a layered frontier in which preservation, interpretation, verification, and compiled realization have different minimal structures. This is an open hypothesis, not a conclusion.
