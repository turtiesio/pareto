# Raw unknown-data pressure corpus

Retain and later reinterpret:

- opaque bytes including empty bytes;
- syntactically distinct but currently equivalent JSON;
- duplicate and unknown JSON fields;
- unknown field names and relation labels;
- future-format payloads;
- unknown actors and protocols;
- truncated and coverage-unknown captures.

Required invariants: original evidence remains unchanged; capture is not called invalid merely because interpretation is absent; no unadmitted interpretation authorizes action; later interpretations identify their method and dependencies.
