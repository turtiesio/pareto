# Hypotheses fixed before testing

## H0 — exact artifact only

A set of bounded exact byte sequences is sufficient. Content hashes may address and deduplicate them.

## H1 — acquisition transcript

At least three roles must remain distinguishable in the preservation apparatus:

1. exact bounded content;
2. occurrence address/multiplicity;
3. exact acquisition context when any was retained, including the distinction between absent context and an empty context artifact.

The context may initially be opaque. Its opacity is counted as deferred interpretation, not semantic simplification.

## H2 — parsed generic structure

A generic map/list/scalar tree can replace the original bytes without relevant loss.

## H3 — raw plus projection

Exact artifacts and acquisition occurrences remain authoritative preservation evidence. Parsed structures are identified, reproducible interpretations or caches linked to that evidence, not replacements for it.

No hypothesis in this round claims that artifact, capture, or interpretation is a final universal semantic primitive.
